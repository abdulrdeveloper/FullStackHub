# 7 Practice Challenges

Build these without using AI first. After you finish all 7, you can use AI to improve your work, add features, or fix mistakes.

1. Create a simple personal profile page with your name, a short intro, an image, and a link to your social media or portfolio.

2. Build a student portfolio page with sections for About, Skills, Projects, and Contact.

3. Make a restaurant menu page with a title, a few food items, prices, and a footer.

4. Create a login form with fields for username and password, plus a submit button.

5. Build a product card for an online shop with an image, product name, price, and a Buy Now button.

6. Make a photo gallery with at least 4 pictures and short captions under each one.

7. Create a simple landing page for a school club, startup, or small business with a heading, description, and call-to-action button.
