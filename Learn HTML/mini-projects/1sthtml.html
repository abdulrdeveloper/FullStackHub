Frequently Asked Questions

Language and Prerequisites?
The course will be taught in Hindi. Technical terms will remain in English. No prior programming experience is required. Basic computer skills and the willingness to learn are enough and explore beyond the course.
Will I get recordings if I miss a class?
Yes. Every session is recorded and uploaded within 3-4 hours. You can watch it anytime until the course access is active.
How long will I have access to the course content?
You get 3 years (1095 days) of access to all videos, notes, and resources.
What is MasterJi and community?
MasterJi is our in-house platform where you track progress, give assignments to submit, provide code feedback and build projects. The community is hosted inside the MasterJi app. You will receive access before the cohort starts.
What projects will I build?
You will build real-world frontend, backend and full-stack projects that are portfolio-ready and reviewed by mentors.
What are the class timings?
Live classes are held on every Saturday & Sunday at 8:00 PM IST, with a bonus & doubt clearing session on every Thursday at 8:00 PM IST.
What payment methods are supported?
All major payment options including UPI apps, Debit/ Credit cards, Netbanking from leading banks and popular wallets.
