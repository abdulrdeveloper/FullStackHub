# Code of Conduct

## Effective Date

August 31, 2026

## Our Standards

Web Dev for All is a welcoming learning space for developers of all backgrounds
and skill levels. Everyone participating in the repository, issues, discussions,
reviews, and pull requests is expected to:

- Be respectful, kind, and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions, different perspectives, and different levels of experience.
- Collaborate in good faith and prioritize shared learning.
- Respect the privacy and personal boundaries of other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's
private information without permission.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open an issue in the
[repository issue tracker](https://github.com/abdulrdeveloper/web-dev-for-all/issues)
with the relevant details. If the report should remain private, contact Abdul
Rahman through the contact details listed in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as private as reasonably possible.
Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope and Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies to all repository spaces and community interactions
related to this project. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or
take other appropriate action when behavior violates these standards.

## Questions

For questions about this Code of Conduct, open an issue using the repository issue
tracker.
